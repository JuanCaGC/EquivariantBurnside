# Conventions

**Characters.** A symbol has `hgens`, a list of elements h_1..h_k of G that are independent generators of an abelian subgroup H (|H| equals the product of the orders of the h_j). A character beta of H is given by integers c = (c_1..c_k) with beta(h_j) = exp(2 pi i c_j / ord(h_j)). Only c_j mod ord(h_j) matters.

**Symbols and the group Y.** Both papers (Kresch-Tschinkel 2105.02929 Definition 8.1, and arXiv 2302.02296 section 3.2) write Y as a subgroup of Z_G(H)/H. The engine and Theorem 5.2 of Tschinkel-Yang-Zhang (arXiv 2112.12801) use the preimage Y~ with H <= Y~ <= Z_G(H). `BCSymbol(G, hgens, ygens, betas)` takes `ygens` in Z_G(H) and means Y~ = <H, ygens>, that is Y = <ygens>H/H, and empty `ygens` means Y trivial. `BCSymbol_preimage(G, hgens, ygens, betas)` takes generators of a subgroup Y~ that must contain H, otherwise `ArgumentError`.

**The block key.** `BlockKey` is (Hdesc, d, Ydesc, Ysize, orbitsize, rank) where Hdesc and Ydesc are GAP StructureDescription strings, d the orders of the independent generators of the representative H, Ysize = |Y~|, orbitsize the size of the N_G(H) orbit of Y~, and rank the 1-based rank of the block among blocks with identical first five entries in the enumeration order of the engine (1 unless two blocks share all invariants).

**Coordinates.** Coordinates of a `BCElement` are in the Smith basis chosen by the engine and are comparable only between elements produced by the same version of the package. Use `==`, `is_zero`, `order` and certificates for exchange.

**Group fingerprint.** `group_fingerprint(G)` is the SHA-256 hex of the text "EB-GROUP-1\n" * order * "\n" * the GAP strings of `GeneratorsOfGroup(G)` joined by newlines. It binds a certificate to the given generating tuple and NOT to the isomorphism type.

**What a non-vanishing certificate does and does not prove.** `verify` checks, in plain integer arithmetic and without Oscar, that the stated functional f kills every relation of B_n(H)/(C) regenerated from d, n and the stated permutations of characters, and that f of the class vector is nonzero. If it returns true the class vector is nonzero in B_n(H)/(C) for those data. It does NOT check that the permutations are the stabilizer action of the group, nor that the symbols map to the class vector. `verify_with_group` rebuilds the group from the certificate and re-derives the block data and the permutations through different OSCAR calls than the engine, but it does not recompute the class vector from the symbols.

**Errors.** The symbol and class constructors throw `ArgumentError` for: H trivial, H not abelian, hgens not independent, a ygens element not centralizing H, preimage form with H not contained in the group generated by ygens, a beta of the wrong length, a zero beta, betas that do not generate the dual of H, more nonzero betas than n, a symbol from a different group, n < 1.

Phase 0 and 1 compute BC_n(G) and images of classes, not classes of varieties.

**The image of a class and the restriction correction.** The group BC_n(G) is the one defined by the relations (O), (C), (V) and (B) of EBGLA section 3.2, where the blowup relation (B) has a second term Theta_2 supported on the subgroup `Ker(b_1 - b_2)` of `H`. `bc_image` computes in the block decomposition of Theorem 5.2 of Tschinkel-Yang-Zhang, in which that term is absent. The two presentations are related by the automorphism `phi(H, Y, beta) = sum over subgroups K <= H with every b_i restricted to K nonzero of (K, Y, beta|_K)` of the group of symbols, triangular with respect to the order of the subgroup, and `bc_image` evaluates `phi` of the class (option `blowup_correction`). A non-vanishing certificate therefore certifies that `phi(class)` is nonzero in the block quotient, which is equivalent to the class being nonzero in BC_n(G). The expansion `phi` itself is part of the trusted code, like the passage from symbols to the class vector. This was checked against the presentation of the definition by `scripts/audit_theta2.jl` for several small groups and for the group of order 192 of EBGLA Example 7.5.


**Actions on the projective line.** A `ProjAction` stores a faithful action of a finite group on `P^1` through matrices in `GL_2(C)` up to scalars. Points are lines `[x0 : x1]` with coordinate `z = x0 / x1`, so `diag(a, 1)` is `z -> a z` and `[[0, a], [1, 0]]` is `z -> a / z`. The action is a left action when the product `g * h` of the group is `g` after `h` (option `convention = :left`). The weight at a fixed point `[v]` with `M v = lambda v` is `det(M) / lambda^2`, the eigenvalue of the differential. The datum of an action up to conjugation in `PGL_2(C)` is the class function `theta` with `exp(2 pi i theta)` the ratio of eigenvalues, defined modulo sign, equivalently `chi(g) = 1 + 2 cos(2 pi theta(g))`, the character of the adjoint representation. It is a complete invariant because `PGL_2(C) = SO_3(C)` and isomorphic orthogonal representations of a finite group are conjugate. The datum is checked against the printed pair of actions of `A_5` (EBGLA Proposition 5.1), see `test/test_projective_line.jl`.

**Incompressible symbols.** `incompressible_class(act)` returns the divisor symbols `(H, Y acting on k(P^1), beta)` of the class of a toric surface for which `Y = Y~/H` is not cyclic. These are the incompressible ones of Kresch-Tschinkel (arXiv:2108.00518), Proposition 3.6, and they generate a direct summand of `Burn_2(G)` free modulo conjugation (Proposition 3.4). Two symbols are equal when an element of `G` conjugates `H`, `Y~`, `beta` and the datum `theta` of one to those of the other. The action of `Y~` on each curve is computed as a matrix: for a boundary divisor `D_rho` by `w -> det(nu) w + det(rho, t)`, for the closure of a fixed layer by the shift along the layer, for an exceptional curve by the differential of the action. The code checks that these matrices form a homomorphism and that the elements acting trivially are exactly `H`. Everything about this class is only as reliable as `toric_class`, whose symbols it reuses: see the validation list in the docstring of `incompressible_class`.

**Indexed symbols.** An indexed symbol `(H in A, Y~, beta, gamma)` of `Burn_{n,I}(G)` is stored in subgroup notation: `H <= A` abelian, `Y~` with `A <= Y~ <= Z_G(A)` (the group `Y = Y~/A` acts faithfully on one component of the field, whose only recorded datum is its transcendence degree `d`), `beta` a list of `n - d - |I|` nonzero characters of `A` trivial on `H` with common kernel `H`, and `gamma` characters of `A` indexed by `I` whose restrictions to `H` generate its dual. Characters are dictionaries from the elements of `A` to `Q/Z`. A class is a formal sum, no relation `(B1)` or `(B2)` is applied, and symbols are identified only by conjugation in `G` and by reordering `beta`. The operation `psi` is Definition 4.2 and `tau` is Example 4.1 of Kresch-Tschinkel (arXiv:2108.00518), `eta` is Definition 5.8 for a central `H` and induction is Definition 3.1. In `tau` a retained index absorbs the omitted indices since the previous retained one, in the direction of the next retained index, and the transformation is applied before `psi`. The operation `omega` (KT-struct Section 4) replaces `A` by the intersection `A'` of the kernels of the removed characters, keeps `Y~` as a subgroup of `G`, restricts `beta` and the retained `gamma` to `A'`, raises `d` by the number of removed indices, and is zero when some restricted character of `beta` vanishes. The restriction to a subgroup `L` (KT-struct Theorem 7.2) is a sum over representatives `k` of the double cosets `L k Y~` of the symbols `(H^g cap L in A^g cap L, Y~^g cap L, beta^g, gamma^g)` with `g = k^-1` and restricted characters, dropping the terms in which a character of `beta` vanishes. The product (KT-struct Section 6) takes direct products of the three subgroups and extends every character by zero. The conjugate of `x` by `g` is `g^-1 x g`.
