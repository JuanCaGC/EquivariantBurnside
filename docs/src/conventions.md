# Conventions

**Characters.** A symbol has `hgens`, a list of elements h_1..h_k of G that are independent generators of an abelian subgroup H (|H| equals the product of the orders of the h_j). A character beta of H is given by integers c = (c_1..c_k) with beta(h_j) = exp(2 pi i c_j / ord(h_j)). Only c_j mod ord(h_j) matters.

**Symbols and the group Y.** Both papers (Kresch-Tschinkel 2105.02929 Definition 8.1, and arXiv 2302.02296 section 3.2) write Y as a subgroup of Z_G(H)/H. The engine and Theorem 5.2 of Tschinkel-Yang-Zhang (arXiv 2112.12801) use the preimage Y~ with H <= Y~ <= Z_G(H). `BCSymbol(G, hgens, ygens, betas)` takes `ygens` in Z_G(H) and means Y~ = <H, ygens>, that is Y = <ygens>H/H, and empty `ygens` means Y trivial. `BCSymbol_preimage(G, hgens, ygens, betas)` takes generators of a subgroup Y~ that must contain H, otherwise `ArgumentError`.

**The block key.** `BlockKey` is (Hdesc, d, Ydesc, Ysize, orbitsize, rank) where Hdesc and Ydesc are GAP StructureDescription strings, d the orders of the independent generators of the representative H, Ysize = |Y~|, orbitsize the size of the N_G(H) orbit of Y~, and rank the 1-based rank of the block among blocks with identical first five entries in the enumeration order of the engine (1 unless two blocks share all invariants).

**Coordinates.** Coordinates of a `BCElement` are in the Smith basis chosen by the engine and are comparable only between elements produced by the same version of the package. Use `==`, `is_zero`, `order` and certificates for exchange.

**Group fingerprint.** `group_fingerprint(G)` is the SHA-256 hex of the text "EB-GROUP-1\n" * order * "\n" * the GAP strings of `GeneratorsOfGroup(G)` joined by newlines. It binds a certificate to the given generating tuple and NOT to the isomorphism type.

**What a non-vanishing certificate does and does not prove.** `verify` checks, in plain integer arithmetic and without Oscar, that the stated functional f kills every relation of B_n(H)/(C) regenerated from d, n and the stated permutations of characters, and that f of the class vector is nonzero. If it returns true the class vector is nonzero in B_n(H)/(C) for those data. It does NOT check that the permutations are the stabilizer action of the group, nor that the symbols map to the class vector. `verify_with_group` rebuilds the group from the certificate and re-derives the block data and the permutations through different OSCAR calls than the engine, but it does not recompute the class vector from the symbols.

**Errors.** The symbol and class constructors throw `ArgumentError` for: H trivial, H not abelian, hgens not independent, a ygens element not centralizing H, preimage form with H not contained in the group generated by ygens, a beta of the wrong length, a zero beta, betas that do not generate the dual of H, more nonzero betas than n, a symbol from a different group, n < 1.

Phase 0 and 1 compute BC_n(G) and images of classes, not classes of varieties.
